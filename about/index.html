About

Research, projects, a portfolio in the open, and essays.

I’m a student at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania. This is where I publish my investment research, the things I build, what I own and longer essays.

What you’ll find here #

Research. Company deep dives, trade ideas and quantitative studies. Each note opens with the call, the share price on the day it was written and what I think the stock is worth, so you can check later how it aged.

Projects. Trading bots and solvers, market simulators, alternative-data pipelines and research tools, each with what it found, including the experiments that found nothing. The public code is on GitHub.

Portfolio. What I own, shown by weight rather than by amount, with links to the thesis behind each position where there is one.

Essays. Longer pieces on markets, companies and ideas.

Ground rules #

  • Numbers carry a date and a source.
  • Nothing here is investment advice, and I’m not a licensed financial adviser.
