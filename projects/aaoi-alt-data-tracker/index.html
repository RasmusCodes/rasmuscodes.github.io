AAOI alt-data tracker

Seven public data series on Applied Optoelectronics and its market, refreshed weekly into tidy files and a model-ready workbook.

RS Rasmus Suonio
2 Nov 2026 · 1 min read
Done Nov 2026
Data series
7
Refresh
Weekly, append-only
  • Python
  • Excel

One command collects seven public data series on Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI) and the market around it, appends anything new to tidy CSV files and rebuilds an Excel workbook that links straight into a valuation model.

The sources include SEC filings, US Census trade data, tariff-code lookups, job postings and Texas construction-project filings for the company’s new fabs, plus a few hand-maintained inputs.

The ground rules are built into the code:

  • official APIs first, and every host’s robots.txt checked on every run;
  • no logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs or rate-limit workarounds;
  • nothing invented, interpolated or estimated: a missing input leaves a blank and a logged reason;
  • every raw response cached with its retrieval time.
