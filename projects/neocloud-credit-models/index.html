Debt-versus-equity models for AI neoclouds

Recovery waterfalls built from the filings, priced against bonds, CDS and deep puts for CoreWeave, Nebius and IREN.

RS Rasmus Suonio
24 Oct 2026 · 1 min read
Done Oct 2026

A set of models that asks one question of each AI cloud company with traded debt: does the debt price more default risk than the equity tail does?

  • Recovery is derived, not assumed. A bottom-up waterfall from each company’s filings: the debt stack and what ranks ahead of the unsecured notes, leases, and the assets left for creditors in a default.
  • Default probabilities from every market. Hazard rates fitted to bond prices from FINRA TRACE, CDS spreads, and deep out-of-the-money puts read as default insurance, following Carr and Wu.
  • Structural and convertible models. A CreditGrades structural model, and a jump-to-default convertible tree calibrated to the listed volatility surface.
  • Trades and scenarios. Position sizing and scenario P&L for each structure, from notes alone to notes hedged with puts and stock.

The cleanest way to state each answer is a breakeven recovery: the recovery at which a bond’s implied default probability equals the default probability in the deepest listed puts.
