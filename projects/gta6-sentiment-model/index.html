GTA VI sentiment model

Google Trends, Reddit and Wikipedia data turned into a launch forecast and a valuation for Take-Two.

RS Rasmus Suonio
28 Oct 2026 · 1 min read
Done Oct 2026
Launch forecast
About 38M copies by Mar 2027
Value against price

How much can online attention say about the biggest game launch in years, and does it matter for the stock?

  • Search: Google Trends interest, chained across five-year windows, puts GTA VI’s share of searches in the weeks before launch level with GTA V’s before its 2013 release.
  • Purchase intent: Reddit comments classified by whether people say they are buying. The “buying” share rose from 61% at the price reveal to 71% in October.
  • Cross-checks: traffic-adjusted Wikipedia pageviews and reported digital pre-order counts.

Those feed a sales projection of about 38 million copies by March 2027 (range 28–48 million) and a discounted-cash-flow model of Take-Two. The launch hardly moves intrinsic value: the probability-weighted value is $124 a share against a $195 price, and the swing factor is GTA VI Online, which has not launched yet.
