Celsius alternative-data terminal

A local pipeline that tracks consumer-demand signals for Celsius brand by brand, because the consolidated number hides the split.

RS Rasmus Suonio
28 Sep 2026 · 1 min read
Done Sep 2026
Observations
21,027
Kept separate
CELSIUS, Alani Nu, Rockstar
  • Python
  • SQLite

Celsius Holdings can grow through its Alani Nu acquisition while the original CELSIUS brand shrinks, and the consolidated revenue line hides exactly that. This pipeline tracks publicly available demand signals for each brand separately, runs locally on a schedule and is built to be read weekly.

  • Data with no keys and no cost: 21,027 observations from Wikipedia pageviews for ten brands since 2018 and SEC filings covering 34 quarters of reported revenue.
  • Ground truth by hand: 78 rows transcribed from five earnings releases, including brand-level retail sales and dollar share from Circana that exist in no API.
  • Outputs: a dashboard, 11 charts, a thesis scorecard and an HTML report.
  • A backtest that says “not enough history” — the correct answer for now, explained rather than hidden.

Six further sources are implemented and switch themselves on when an API key is added. Four more are registered but deliberately not implemented, each with the reason.
