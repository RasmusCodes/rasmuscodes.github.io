Industrial Intelligence

Public operational data (factory orders, power-plant pipelines, grid demand, customers' capital spending, construction registrations and tenders) mapped to industrial companies and tested, point in time, against what the companies later reported.

Revenue segments

Quarterly revenue by reportable segment from the companies' XBRL filings, latest reported values.

Operational indicators against reported revenue

Each indicator was linked to the company from its own business description before any model was fitted. Growth rates are year over year, as first published.

Forecast and assumptions

Revenue for the next three quarters from the partially pooled indicator model, fitted on every quarter reported so far. Change the indicator assumptions to see the trajectory move.

Historical forecast errors

Walk-forward: every forecast uses only data public one second before the company's release, with models refit each quarter.

Contracts, projects and announcements

Company press releases (8-K), federal contract awards, large planned power plants and Texas data center registrations, each with its source.

    What the tests found, all companies

    Out-of-sample accuracy of revenue-growth forecasts; 2016–2020 development period and 2021 onward holdout. Lower error is better; ratios below 1 beat the baseline.

    Data provenance

    Every source, how its point-in-time date is set and whether it was used. "Revised data, lagged" means only today's revised history exists, so revisions are not point in time.
