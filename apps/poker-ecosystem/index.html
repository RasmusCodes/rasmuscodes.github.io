Poker research lab

Solve a river or turn spot range against range, measure exactly how far the answer is from equilibrium, then tell the solver how an opponent really plays and compare the equilibrium strategy with exploitative ones. Everything runs in your browser on a JavaScript port of the Rust engine, checked against it to the last digit.

The model starts from the equilibrium and replaces the covered decisions with threshold play on the range reaching them: raise the strongest share, fold the weakest, bet value from the top and bluffs from the bottom. p = 0 is the equilibrium, p = 1 the best response to the model.

Ranges, street by street model-based estimate

What the public actions say about each player's cards. Every action is evidence: a model built from the player's frequencies in this database (shrunk towards the population) says which part of their range takes it, with noise for slow-plays and floats. Shared cards are removed as they are dealt.

Heads-up solves are equilibrium approximations of the game defined by the bet sizes shown, with exploitability computed exactly in that game. Multiway results in the research reports are CFR approximations without an equilibrium guarantee. The opponent population is simulated. Built by Rasmus Suonio; source code.
