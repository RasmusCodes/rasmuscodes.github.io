Does smart order routing pay?

A simulated market with four exchanges and a dark pool, each with its own fees, queues and distance from the router. The Python simulator runs in your browser: pick a market, an order and the routers to compare.

The demo needs JavaScript.

Simulation only: fictional venues and agents, nothing here describes live trading. The engine is the repository's Python package, run by Pyodide (CPython compiled to WebAssembly).
