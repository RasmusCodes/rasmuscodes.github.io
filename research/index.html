Netflix: the world is still switching, and the price assumes it has stopped

BUY with a 12-month target of $71, the probability-weighted DCF value. At $60.75 the market implies Netflix’s share of broadband homes outside the US & Canada falls from 34% to about 27% by 2035; the base case has it rising to 41%.

Does Netflix trade flat between earnings?

Earnings days are 1.6% of trading days but carry 28% of the variance. Between prints NFLX runs at about 36% annualised volatility, and neither half produced an edge after costs.
